HOW I BUILD

Fewer features, fewer promises.

Offline is the default
If a feature only works with a good connection, it shouldn't be the one people depend on.
Say what the OS controls
Focus modes, battery savers and alarm permissions are the phone's call. The UI explains that rather than over-promising.
Three languages from day one
English, French and Arabic — RTL designed properly, not a mirrored afterthought.
Accessible by default
Large text, high contrast and 48px touch targets are part of the first draft, not a later pass.
Your data stays yours
No account unless a feature genuinely needs one, and nothing leaves the device without being asked.
Ship, then listen
Small releases, real users, and changes based on what actually breaks in their hands.
